Put your own legally-owned audio files here to play them during the
"Bring Sally Up" challenges.

Expected filename: bring_sally_up.mp3
(accepted formats: .mp3, .m4a, .wav, .ogg)

TABATA challenges do NOT use background music in this app -- they run on
the timer only (with the exercise photos), since syncing music to the
work/hold intervals gets too complex with the images.

Note: this app cannot include the actual song file for you -- add your own
copy (bought/streamed legally) to this folder.
